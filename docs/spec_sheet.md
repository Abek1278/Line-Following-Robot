### Notes & Tolerances
- **M2 Screw Clearance:** Typically 2.2 – 2.4 mm printed hole diameter.
- **M3 Screw Clearance:** Typically 3.2 – 3.4 mm printed hole diameter.
- **Sensor ground clearance target:** 3.0 to 5.0 mm.
- **Chassis ground clearance target:** Minimum 2.0 to 3.0 mm.
